# Fixture credits

The `showcase-*` media below are real, freely licensed works, used by the
documentation screenshots. Every other fixture in this folder is made for the
tests.

CC0 is the [CC0 1.0 Universal public domain dedication][cc0]. The Unsplash
photos were published there under CC0 before Unsplash changed its licence in
June 2017, and are kept on Wikimedia Commons under that dedication.
